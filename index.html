The Story
Behind the Work

Lisa is a Porto-based stunt performer and action actress with credits spanning Portuguese, American, and Australian productions — from national TV commercials to international drama series and reality sports formats. Her on-screen work is grounded in a background in martial arts, precision movement, and years of competitive physical training.

Holding Graded Stunt Performer status under Australian industry standards, Lisa has built her career through elite physical discipline and meticulous preparation. The majority of her credited work has been coordinated through Campground Stunts (Carlos Pereira), and she is actively expanding into the European film markets of Madrid and Sofia.

Her approach to every production is the same: arrive prepared, understand the story being told, and execute with the precision that keeps everyone on set safe and every frame worth watching.
