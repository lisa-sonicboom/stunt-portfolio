About

Trained to take the hit — and make it look effortless.

Lisa Koivunen is a MEAA-graded stunt performer and lifelong martial artist working across Spain and Portugal, and available worldwide.

Her work spans fight choreography, falls and high falls, and wire work — built on a foundation of taekwondo, parkour, and relentless physical conditioning. From doubling on television to commercials and rigging, she brings energy, precision and professionalism to every set.
